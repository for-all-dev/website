Matthew Doty has been doing formal methods since 2008, when he formalized a complete proof of the Robbin’s Conjecture. Since then he has formally verified results in lattice theory, and original research into probability theory, complexity theory, rational decision making and finance. He is the coauthor of the Ethereum mining algorithm with Vitalik Buterin, and has worked at NASA on the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Sattelite (TESS), a space mission to find planets around other stars. He holds an MSc in Mathematical Logic from Universiteit van Amsterdam.
